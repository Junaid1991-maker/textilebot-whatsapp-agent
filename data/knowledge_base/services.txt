**Company Profile: TextileBot Export Services**

**Introduction**

TextileBot Export Services is a renowned Pakistani textile exporter specializing in the design, manufacturing, and export of a diverse range of high-quality textiles and garments. Headquartered in Lahore, Pakistan, the company has established itself as a leading player in the global textile market, catering to discerning clients across the world.

**Products and Capabilities**

Our extensive product portfolio includes a variety of fabrics, such as cotton, polyester, linen, and blends, as well as a range of garments encompassing casual wear, formal wear, and home textiles. Our capabilities encompass:

* Design and development of bespoke textiles and garments
* Manufacturing of high-quality fabrics and finished goods
* Export of textiles and garments to multiple countries worldwide
* Supply chain management and logistics expertise

**Certifications and Quality Standards**

TextileBot Export Services holds esteemed certifications from leading international organizations, including:

* **OEKO-TEX Standard 100**: We meet the rigorous standards for human-ecological safety and sustainable textile production.
* **Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS)**: Our commitment to organic and sustainable practices ensures our certified organic textiles meet the highest international standards.
* **Global Recycled Standard (GRS)**: Our expertise in recycling and upcycling ensures our GRS-certified products meet the highest requirements for sustainability.

Our quality standards are based on ISO 9001:2015 and we maintain a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, with a focus on product quality, on-time delivery, and excellent customer service.

**Production Capacity**

Our state-of-the-art production facilities and skilled workforce enable us to produce high-quality textiles and garments to meet the demands of our global clientele. With an available production capacity of 500,000 meters per day, we can cater to large-scale orders while maintaining flexibility and responsiveness.
